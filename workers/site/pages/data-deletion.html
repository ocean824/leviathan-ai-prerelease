You can ask us to delete your Leviathan AI account and personal data. Email us to request deletion. There is no self-service deletion form yet.

  1. In Leviathan AI, disable automation and cancel any active subscription. Confirm that no open order or account action depends on the connection.
  2. Remove direct broker and data-provider credentials from the app's Connections settings. Those credentials are stored in the device's Windows DPAPI or macOS Keychain store; remove them from every device where you configured them.
  3. If Google sign-in is enabled for your account, you may also revoke Leviathan AI in your Google Account's third-party connections.
  4. If SnapTrade is enabled, disconnect each brokerage in Leviathan AI or SnapTrade's connection controls.
  5. Send a request from the account email to {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}} with the subject “Leviathan AI data deletion.” Include your account email and a brief request. Never include your password, tokens, keys or full payment-card number.
  6. We will verify you control the account, confirm the request, and delete or de-identify eligible active account data. Data retained for legal, accounting, security or dispute purposes will be restricted and kept only as long as necessary.

We process verified deletion requests through an operator-run process. We delete or irreversibly de-identify eligible account records within {{RETENTION_DELETION_DAYS}} days.
