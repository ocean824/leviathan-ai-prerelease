The providers below may process information when their related service is used. Availability and data flow depend on which platform features you enable.

  • Cloudflare — Workers hosting and delivery for licensing, the public legal site, and planned Cloudflare R2 storage for per-user strategy-library files. The web app’s hosting provider will be listed here before launch.
  • Neon — PostgreSQL hosting for licensing and account records, with per-user sync state planned for settings, strategies, watchlists and alerts.
  • Stripe — subscription checkout and payment processing. Card entry is handled by Stripe.
  • Google — identity provider for Google sign-in, which is being introduced.
  • SnapTrade — brokerage connectivity for connections you choose to make through SnapTrade (being introduced).
  • Configured AI providers: Anthropic, OpenAI and the OpenAI-compatible endpoint you choose (including a local endpoint) — the provider you choose in the application receives the prompts and context you submit to its AI feature. Provider identity is selected by you in product configuration.
  • Hosted Python API provider — The web app’s Python API will run in a container hosted by {{WEB_BACKEND_HOST}}.
  • Market-data vendors — Databento, Massive and Unusual Whales may supply licensed data when their respective integrations are enabled. Public government and exchange sources may also be queried. The documented flow does not send personal account information to market-data vendors.

Provider terms and privacy policies govern their services. We may update this list when providers or integrations change.
