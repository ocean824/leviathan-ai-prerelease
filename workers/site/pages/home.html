Leviathan AI is a trading and analysis platform for Windows and macOS. It brings market research, strategy development, portfolio and risk workflows, and user-directed execution into a desktop workspace.

Research and analysis

Explore market data, indicators, strategy ideas, charts, scenarios and historical simulations. Data availability depends on the sources and entitlements you configure.

Risk controls and settings

Review risk inputs and execution controls, and inspect why a proposal was produced. Availability and behavior depend on your platform version, connected venue and configuration.

Trading modes

Analysis is for research and does not place orders. Semi-automatic workflows request approval for each proposed order. Full automation is separately enabled by the user and operates only within configured rules and available safeguards.

Leviathan AI does not promise returns or trading performance. Trading can result in partial or total loss. Read the risk disclosure and terms.
